# Blurry is honor system: as it's cast, every player (any of them could be the defending player later,
# e.g. after a control change) says whether they were wearing glasses, and the ones who were are
# remembered. Only their creatures can block it. Put onto the battlefield without being cast, it can't
# be blocked at all (Unstable ruling) - the !wasCast static covers a flickered Beeble too, since the
# card copy made on leaving the battlefield carries its remembered players along.
